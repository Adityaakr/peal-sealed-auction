Here is what one bid puts on chain. The browser packs quantity and price into thirty-two bytes, and encrypts them under a fresh key, K. K is split into three shares. Each share is sealed with M L K E M ten twenty-four, to one member's key registered on chain. The contract stores the ciphertext and the hashes, and holds the escrow. There is no reveal step, so a bidder cannot back out after seeing the others.
