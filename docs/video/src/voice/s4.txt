This is the live site, on the Core Chain testnet. A visitor creates a guest account in the browser, and funds it with test Core. On the bid page, it checks every committee member: the M L D S A eighty-seven key from the chain, the encryption key against the hash the member signed, and its card. Three green checks. Then: seal in this browser, and commit. The page registers the guest's post-quantum key, opens one E V M window scoped to the three contracts it calls, and sends the faucet claim, the approval, and the sealed bid. Three envelopes, seventeen hundred and fifty-two bytes each. Nobody can read it.
